# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

## About Reel Uploader

Reel Uploader is a privately operated application used by its owner and authorized administrators to publish and manage authorized content on Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts. It is not offered as a public software-as-a-service product.

## Information accessed

When an authorized administrator connects a Meta account, Facebook Page, or Instagram account, Reel Uploader may access information made available through Meta APIs that is necessary to:

- authenticate the administrator and confirm authorization;
- identify the connected account, Facebook Page, or Instagram account;
- determine the permissions granted to the application; and
- upload, publish, or manage content requested by the authorized administrator.

This may include Meta user, Page, or Instagram account identifiers and names, granted permission information, and information about content handled through the application. The exact information available is controlled by Meta and by the permissions granted to Reel Uploader.

## Access tokens and credentials

Access tokens and other credentials are used only to make authorized requests to Meta APIs. They are treated as sensitive and are not intentionally made public. Administrators should not send tokens, passwords, or other credentials through public channels.

## Use and disclosure

Information accessed through Meta APIs is used to authenticate and authorize access, identify connected accounts, and perform the requested content-publishing or management actions. Personal information is not sold. Reel Uploader does not use Meta account information for advertising or unrelated tracking.

Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policies. Reel Uploader does not control Meta's processing of information.

## Data choices

An administrator can revoke Reel Uploader's access through the relevant Facebook or Instagram settings. Requests to delete data associated with Reel Uploader can be made by following the [Data Deletion Instructions](../data-deletion/).

## Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's operation or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
